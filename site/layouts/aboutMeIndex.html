In recent years, I have focused primarily on research. I completed my doctorate in 2025 with a dissertation on hybrid rendering models in web application development. My work explores rendering, web performance, and AI in development and education. I also advise MSc and BSc theses at Aalto University. [Explore my research](/research/) or [browse my talks](/presentations/). I developed my first website around 1996 although it has been lost to the history and I was not serious about web development back then. It was only in early 2000s that I started to become more serious about programming as I became involved with the [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) project for several years starting from 2005 as I contributed to the project by coding and improving documentation. I was one of the founders of [webpack](https://webpack.js.org/) core team and helped to establish the project to a broader public through [my webpack book](/books/webpack/). I have also authored numerous [open source](/open-source/) projects primarily for my own purposes, although several have become popular on their own as other people and companies have found my work useful. I established my own company in 2016 to support my publishing efforts. I use the company for occasional [consulting](/consulting/) while giving [workshops](/workshops) and [presentations](/presentations/) as opportunities arise. I have been organizing conferences primarily in Finland starting from 2018 as the first [React Finland](https://react-finland.fi/) conference took place then. Since that time, we have pivoted into a new brand called [Future Frontend](https://futurefrontend.com/) to allow discussion about future-oriented web topics beyond React. You can consider this site as my collection of web related knowledge. It now centers on my research and its connection to practical web development.
